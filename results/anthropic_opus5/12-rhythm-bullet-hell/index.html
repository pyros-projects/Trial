the deal

Every drum hit fires bullets. Every bullet is scheduled on the same musical transport as the soundtrack, so the pattern is the beat. Move with WASD/arrows, hold Shift to focus (slow + visible hitbox), hold Z to fire. Act on the beat — shots, dashes and grazes landing inside the beat window score PERFECT, build combo and refill energy. Ignore the rhythm and you can still win; ride it and you'll double your score.
Audio requires a click — browsers block sound until you interact.
