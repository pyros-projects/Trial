Pointer

Drag empty space to pan · Wheel / pinch to zoom about the cursor · Click a body to select it · while paused, drag a spacecraft to move it, or drag the arrow head of its velocity vector to re-aim the burn-free trajectory · drag a maneuver node marker along the prediction to re-time the burn.

Units

Normalised, internally coherent units: length LU, mass MU, time TU, with G configurable (default 1). Each scenario declares how many seconds of mission clock one TU represents so the MET readout stays human-readable. Velocities are LU/TU, energies are per unit mass (LU²/TU²), angular momentum LU²/TU.

Model notes

Full N-body: every body attracts every other body, spacecraft included. The simulation is planar (2-D), so maneuver components are prograde / retrograde, radial in / out and Cartesian — a normal (out-of-plane) component has no meaning in the plane and is therefore not offered. Sphere-of-influence circles, osculating conics and closest-approach markers are diagnostic overlays computed from the true integrated state, not the model itself.
