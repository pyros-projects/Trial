VELOCIRAPTOR FPV

A physically simulated FPV quad — motor mixing, prop lag, quaternion attitude, and a deterministic procedural course. You are parked 8 m behind the glowing START/FINISH gate. Hold W to spool the motors and fly through it.

Left stick — power & heading

W/S throttle up/down · A/D yaw left/right. Throttle is the only thing holding you up.

Right stick — attitude

↑ nose down (accelerate forward) · ↓ nose up (brake) · ←/→ roll. Tilt is how you move.
