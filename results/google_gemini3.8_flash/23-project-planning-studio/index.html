Scheduling Limits & Policy

Supported Workspace Limits

  • Tasks: Up to 500 tasks (IDs: ASCII alphanumeric, _, -).
  • Resources: Up to 50 resources; integer capacity 1 through 4.
  • Task Duration: Safe integer 0 through 260 working days (0 = milestone).
  • Task Priority: Safe integer 0 through 9999 (lowest numeric value placed first).
  • Calendar Range: 2000-01-01 through 2099-12-31 (Monday-Friday calendar, no holidays).
  • Schedule Horizon: Working-day offsets 0 through 2600 inclusive.

Serial Deterministic Scheduling Policy

  1. Among ready unscheduled tasks whose predecessors have all finished, choose the lowest numeric priority, then tie-break by lexicographically smallest stable ID (ASCII code-point order).
  2. Candidate boundary is max(0, notBeforeOffset, all predecessors' placed finish offsets).
  3. Search from that boundary for the earliest consecutive working-day interval where resource occupancy + 1 never exceeds capacity. Earlier free gaps are filled as well as space after the last booking.
  4. Unassigned tasks need no slot search; duration-zero milestones use their candidate boundary and have no resource.

Dependency-Only CPM Float

Calculated assuming unlimited resources and ignoring not-before dates. Zero-float tasks and critical edges (EF = ES) highlight the purely topological critical path. Actual schedule delays caused by resource waiting or date constraints are tracked and explained separately.
