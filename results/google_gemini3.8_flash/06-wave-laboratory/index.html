Wave Interference & Diffraction Laboratory

Welcome to the Wave Laboratory, an interactive real-time 2D scalar wave equation simulator solving the partial differential equation:

∂²ψ/∂t² + 2γ ∂ψ/∂t = c²(x,y) ∇²ψ + S(x,y,t)

Waves naturally undergo interference, diffraction, reflection, refraction, and absorption directly from the numerical field solver.

Key Features & Tools

  • Inspect Tool: Click and drag sources or measurement probes around the field.
  • Barrier & Absorber Brushes: Draw reflecting mirrors (Dirichlet ψ=0) or anechoic absorbing sponges.
  • Refractive Medium & Lenses: Paint arbitrary regions of high/low index n or stamp biconvex lenses.
  • Oscilloscope & FFT: Place probes to measure real-time waveforms ψ(t), peak amplitudes, relative phases, and live frequency spectra.
  • Phased Array Elements: Steer directional wave beams constructively at specific angles.

Keyboard Shortcuts

Space: Play / Pause F: Single simulation step C: Clear field displacement R: Reset current preset Ctrl+Z: Undo structure edit
