Mouse & Pointer Controls
  • Left Click on tile: Safe move or path preview (halts immediately if danger/enemy enters sight).
  • Left Click on adjacent enemy: Perform melee attack.
  • Left Click on distant enemy: Target with selected ranged weapon or ability.
  • Hover: Inspect tile properties, entity stats, and monster tactical intent.
Tactical Tips
  • Walls and closed doors block line of sight. Lure dangerous archers around corners into melee range.
  • Sound ripples (!) indicate unseen enemies walking or triggering traps in nearby rooms.
  • Conserve healing potions and use class abilities strategically.
  • Reach Floor 5 and defeat the Dread Overlord to obtain the Amulet of Yendor!
