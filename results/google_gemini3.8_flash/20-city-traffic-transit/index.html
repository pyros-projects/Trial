Controls & Navigation

  • Pan Map: Click and drag in Select mode, or Right-Click / Middle-Click drag in any tool. Touch drag on mobile.
  • Zoom: Mouse wheel, pinch gesture, or bottom bar +/- buttons.
  • Continuous Road Drawing: Click start point, move mouse to preview, click to place. Automatically snaps to nearby nodes and splits intersecting roads cleanly. Hold Shift for 45°/90° alignment.
  • Create Transit Route: Select Transit Route Tool, click bus stops in desired sequence to form a line. MetroFlow computes paths and dispatches scheduled buses!
⚖️ Multimodal Comparison: Car-Only vs Transit

Test the macro-economic and congestion effect of public transit. By toggling transit on or off, you can compare network delay, travel times, emissions, and gridlock.

Without Transit (100% Cars)

• All commuters drive private vehicles
• Road bottlenecks saturate rapidly
• Higher queue buildup and carbon emissions

With High-Capacity Transit

• Buses carry up to 40-80 commuters each
• Road corridors stay free-flowing
• Reduced overall network travel time
