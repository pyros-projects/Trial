SDF & CSG Studio - Controls & Shortcuts

Camera Navigation:

  • Left Drag: Orbit camera around focal point
  • Shift + Left Drag or Middle Drag: Pan camera
  • Mouse Wheel / Pinch: Zoom in / out
  • F Key: Focus camera on selected object
  • R Key: Reset camera view

Editing & Selection:

  • Left Click Surface: Pick and select SDF primitive directly
  • 3D Gizmo Arrows: Click and drag Red (X), Green (Y), or Blue (Z) handles to translate in 3D
  • D Key: Duplicate selected object
  • Delete / Backspace: Delete selected object
  • 1 - 8 Keys: Quick switch visualization modes

Constructive Solid Geometry:

Objects are evaluated in order from top to bottom. Smooth operations blend shape boundaries and material properties seamlessly using polynomial smin.
