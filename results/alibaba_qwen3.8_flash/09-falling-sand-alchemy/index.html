Material notes

Salt dissolves into brine that freezes at −6 °C. Lava flashes water to steam and quench-cracks into stone. Acid eats metal, wood and stone (not glass or plastic) and releases hydrogen. A charged wire arcs across a one-cell gap and detonates gunpowder. Plants drink water and grow upward when it is warm. Explosions push a real pressure ring through loose material.
