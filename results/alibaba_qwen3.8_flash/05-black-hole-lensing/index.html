WebGL2 is not available

This explorer renders the black hole by integrating light paths on the GPU, which needs WebGL2 with float render targets. Nothing was faked: the image below is not a pre-rendered picture that could be shown instead.

What to try

  • Enable hardware acceleration in your browser settings and restart the browser.
  • Check chrome://gpu (or about:support in Firefox) for WebGL2 support.
  • Update the graphics driver, or close other GPU-heavy applications.
  • On a remote/virtual machine, install a GPU-capable display driver or use a machine with a real GPU.
