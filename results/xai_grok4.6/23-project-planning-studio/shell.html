Calendar dates 2000-01-01–2099-12-31 (weekends move forward to Monday). Working-day offsets 0–2600 inclusive; a positive task may finish at 2600. Duration 0–260, priority 0–9999, resource capacity 1–4. Up to 200 tasks and 16 resources. No holidays. Monday–Friday only. Edits that cannot fit the horizon or calendar are rejected in full.

Serial placement: among ready tasks, lowest priority then smallest ID. Candidate start is max(0, not-before offset, predecessor finishes). Then the earliest consecutive free resource interval of that duration is used; gaps before the last booking are eligible. Already placed work stays fixed. Dependency-only CPM uses durations and finish-to-start edges with unlimited resources and no date constraints — it is not resource-constrained float.
