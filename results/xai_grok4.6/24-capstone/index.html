About this idea

Ut Pendet is a hanging-model workshop. You pin abutments, hang weights on a net of cables, and a linear force-density solver finds the unique funicular shape. A reciprocal force diagram sits beside the model: corresponding segments stay parallel to the cables, and their lengths are the forces. Invert the hanging chain and the same geometry stands as a compression vault — Hooke’s line, made playable.

Why this

Gaudí’s attic of hanging chains is usually a photograph. Graphic-statics studios usually live inside CAD. This file keeps the physical metaphor (weights, invert, pole as thrust) and the actual equilibrium in one offline page.

Difference from nearby work

Trial’s deformable-physics brief is a cloth/rope playground with grabbing and tearing. This is not that: there is no collision, cutting, or strain integration. Joints are pins; members carry axial force; the shape is Schek’s linear solve; the second canvas is a Cremona-style force diagram. Closest online work (eQuilibrium, PolyFrame, compas-FoFin, PushMePullMe) is CAD or a general physics engine. Here the product is the hanging attic itself.

Limitations

Not structural advice. No buckling, masonry friction, section sizes, or 3D vaults. Self-weight is lumped at joints. A true catenary needs uniform weight along length; this polygonal funicular matches that only in the many-joint limit. Failed imports leave the current model in place.
