How Afterlight works

Act during a short loop. When time expires — or when you press Loop — the world resets and an echo replays your recorded inputs on the same physics timeline. Echoes stand on plates, carry cubes, and throw; you move independently beside them.

Move Arrows / WASD / stick · Jump Z / Space / A · Grab/Throw X / J · Loop R · Pause Esc · Interact also E

Coyote time and jump buffer make ledge jumps forgiving. Down+jump drops through one-way platforms. Gamepad is supported. Timeline scrub while paused is preview-only so echoes stay in sync.
