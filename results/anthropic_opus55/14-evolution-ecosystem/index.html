Evolution Lab — how it works

Organisms are agents with a 23-gene genome. Each tick they sense (5 food/obstacle rays, nearby threats, prey, carrion and mates via a spatial hash, temperature and light), score eight behaviours with inherited weights, steer, pay basal + movement + sensing costs, digest food with diet-dependent efficiency, age, and reproduce with mutation and optional crossover. Roles (herbivore, omnivore, predator, scavenger) are read from the evolving diet and aggression genes. Dead bodies become carrion, carrion and waste become detritus, and soil microbes turn detritus back into fertility for plants.
