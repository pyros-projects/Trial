Help & shortcuts
  • Space pause/resume · . single step · R reset · L lightning
  • V/Shift+V next/prev visualization · C next camera preset
  • Orbit: drag · Pan: right-drag / Shift+drag / two fingers · Zoom: wheel / pinch
  • F free-fly: drag to look, W A S D move, Q/E down/up, Shift fast
  • 1–9 pick a tool (1 = navigate) · [/] brush radius
  • With a brush tool, drag on the terrain (or the map) to paint; hold Alt to orbit instead.

Model: 3D Boussinesq atmosphere, semi-Lagrangian advection, pressure projection, Kessler-type warm/cold microphysics with latent heat, rain sedimentation & evaporation, surface energy and moisture fluxes, soil moisture, snow and runoff, Coriolis, terrain as solid obstacles plus orographic forcing, and a charge proxy that drives lightning.
