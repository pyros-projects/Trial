How to play

Goal: descend five floors and slay The Hollow King. The world is strictly turn-based: nothing moves until you act, and every action below costs exactly one turn. Rejected actions (walking into a wall, out-of-range shots) cost nothing.

Turn rules

Move/attack (bump), open a door (bump), wait, search, pick up, use/throw an item, equip, drop, fire, ability, interact and descend each take 1 action. After your action every enemy (in id order) gains energy by its speed and acts once per 100 energy — fast Ash Jackals act 3 times per 2 turns, Ogre Brutes 4 times per 5. Then fire, gas and smoke tick, then statuses and cooldowns. Hasted: 2 actions per turn; Slowed: 1 action per 2 turns; Stunned: lose actions.

Combat: hit chance = 70% + 5% × (accuracy − evasion), clamped 5–95%. Armor absorbs a random 0–armor per hit (half on crits). Crits double damage (daggers ×3). Attacking an unaware enemy (asleep, idle, investigating) is a sneak attack: automatic hit and critical. Hover or inspect enemies to see exact odds.

Perception: walls, closed doors, tall grass and smoke block sight both ways. Enemies only know where you are when they can see you (or hear noise); otherwise they hunt your last known position. Sounds you hear from unseen places appear as rings on the map and in the log.

Controls click “Rebind”, then press a key (Esc cancels)

Pointer: click a visible or remembered tile to walk there along a previewed safe path (stops when new danger appears); click an adjacent enemy to attack; right-click or long-press to inspect. Touch: tap once to preview a path, tap again to go. In targeting: click a target or use arrows/Tab, then Enter; Esc cancels.
