DEFORMABLELAB — controls

A real-time XPBD (extended position-based dynamics) solver. Every object is a set of particles joined by distance, shear, bending, area, pressure, pin and attachment constraints; collisions are resolved between particles, constraint edges and static geometry every substep.

Ropes and chains snap their ends to whatever is under the pointer: a particle becomes an attachment constraint, an obstacle becomes an anchor, empty space becomes a world pin (rope start only). Cloth tears when a constraint's stretch exceeds the tear threshold; balloons pop when their skin tears or is cut.
