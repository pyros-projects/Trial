Physics: null geodesics of a Schwarzschild metric integrated with RK4 (exact orbit equation u″+u=3Mu²), plus a gravitomagnetic frame-dragging term calibrated to Kerr equatorial shadow edges. Disk emission uses a Novikov–Thorne temperature profile, blackbody colour, and the full Kerr circular-orbit redshift g = 1/[uᵗ(1−Ωλ)] with I ∝ g⁴.
