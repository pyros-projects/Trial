Systems cheat-sheet

Running is fast but loud · Walking is faint · Sneaking is silent, harder to see, and hides you behind desks.
Dark areas cut guard detection sharply — unless you're inside a flashlight beam.
Closed doors block sight and muffle sound. Guards notice doors you leave open.
Guards hunt your last known position, then search nearby, radio others, and eventually return to duty.
