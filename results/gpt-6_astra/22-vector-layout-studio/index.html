Use ⌘ in place of Ctrl on Mac. Drag the pen to create an anchor with Bézier handles. Click the first anchor to close; Enter or Finish path completes an open path. In Node mode, pick a point to expose its local coordinates. A corner handle scales about the selection center; the round handle rotates.

Text uses system font advances, a 0.8 em baseline, and a 1 em first-line box. Line height is a multiplier. Text stays plain text; use Apply text (or Ctrl Enter) to commit and Escape to cancel.

Bounds exclude strokes and control handles. We transform the corners of each leaf’s local geometry box; groups use their union. Hidden geometry keeps its bounds. Group only contiguous siblings. Locked descendants prevent parent edits.

Limits: 200 items · 32 anchors per path · 4 nested groups · 2,000 characters per text · 100 undo steps · 2 MiB project JSON. Coordinates ±100,000; document-space bounds ±1,000,000. Positive uniform scale 0.001–1,000. No arbitrary SVG or raster import. Appearance applies to leaves; groups retain full opacity for reversible ungrouping.

Your files, your session. Download a project and reopen it to continue later. No storage or network is used. New, import, and composition loading clear history. Reset restores Form & field and cancels pending work; it cannot be undone.
