THE FLUID LABORATORY

A small gesture. A ripple of color.

Drag across the canvas to add color and momentum. A quick stroke makes a strong current; a gentle one makes a soft swirl. Touch works too, including multiple fingers.

LOOK BENEATH THE SURFACE

Switch views to explore velocity, pressure, swirling motion, and the small amount of divergence left after pressure correction. Every view shows the same evolving fluid.

FIND YOUR FLOW

Raise viscosity for smoother, thicker currents. Raise vorticity for tighter eddies. Clear dye keeps the momentum; reset restores the original scene and settings. While paused, brush strokes wait until you resume.
