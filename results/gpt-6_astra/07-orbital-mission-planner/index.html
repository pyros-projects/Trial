A field guide to Orbital

Explore the system

Click a body in the list or on the map to inspect it. Drag empty space to pan; use the scroll wheel or pinch to zoom. Focus a body with F, or fit the complete system with the expand control.

Plan a maneuver

Select a spacecraft and choose Add maneuver. Adjust its mission time and signed delta-v. The gold path shows the flight with your burns; the mint path is the unpowered prediction. Prograde follows the spacecraft’s velocity relative to its primary; radial points outward. Negative values are retrograde or inward. Cartesian burns use inertial X/Y. This is a 2D sandbox, so out-of-plane burns are not available.

You can also double-click the predicted path, or press M and click a future point. Drag a gold node along the trajectory to change its time. Resume playback to execute the burn automatically. The flight log records its actual impulse.

Edit and experiment

Pause to drag your selected spacecraft’s position or the circular tip of its velocity arrow. Edit initial state sets any body’s mass, radius, display scale, position and velocity. The plus beside Celestial system adds another spacecraft.

Reference frames

Inertial uses the system’s coordinates. The primary-centered view follows the selected body’s primary; Selected body centers the selection itself. The rotating frame follows the primary and aligns its moon to the positive X axis. All history, predictions and vectors are transformed at their own timestamps, including the rotational velocity term.

Physics and units

DU (distance), TU (time) and MU (mass) are coherent normalized units, not kilometers, seconds or Earth masses. All active bodies exert gravity on each other. Velocity Verlet uses a configurable maximum step with shorter steps near encounters and burns. Softening is included in both force and total energy.

Orbital elements and sphere-of-influence boundaries are two-body approximations in an N-body system. Closest approaches are sampled estimates within the prediction horizon. Energy and momentum drift exclude the intentional changes caused by burns, collisions and state edits. High warp may run slower than requested when the step budget is reached; the displayed achieved warp reports this.

Keep your work

Save checkpoints to rewind an experiment. Restart returns to the loaded scenario. Save mission stores this workspace locally; JSON export and import provide portable copies. Autosave restores paused on reload when browser storage is available.
