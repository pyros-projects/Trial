VISUALS & ACCESSIBILITY

Settings save automatically on this device. Opening a panel pauses the encounter.

A little rhythm goes a long way.

01 / Survive the signal

Move with WASD or arrows. Your ship fires automatically at the boss. Only the small center point takes damage. Hold Shift to slow down and see it clearly.

02 / Dash through the drop

Space dashes in your movement direction, protects you briefly, and clears nearby bullets. It recharges every four beats. Perfect timing clears a larger area and grows your combo.

03 / Make the beat count

Tap J for a rhythm pulse. Perfect: within 50 ms of a beat. Good: within 110 ms. Grazing a bullet close to the beat also earns a bonus. Damage breaks your combo. Offbeat dashes still protect you.

04 / Shape your own storm

The pattern lab lets you change tempo, speed, density, subdivisions, and a sixteen-step emission grid. Lab damage restores your lives so you can keep practicing.

The encounter lasts 24 measures with three musical phases. Survive to contain the core, or shoot it down in the final phase. P / Escape pauses, R restarts. Drag to move on touch screens; the buttons below the arena handle focus, dash, and pulse. Replays use the same fixed-step simulation and seed.
