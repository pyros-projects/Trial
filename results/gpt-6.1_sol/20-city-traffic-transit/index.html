A small guide to your city lab.

Build & explore

Choose Draw road, then drag a continuous path. Roads snap to nearby junctions and split at crossings. Select a road to change its lanes, direction, speed, or closure. Use land use to create real origins and destinations.

Select a vehicle to see its destination and planned path. Alt-drag or the Move map tool pans; the mouse wheel or + / − zooms. Two fingers pan and zoom on touch screens.

Signals & transit

Click an intersection with Traffic signal. Its inspector includes phase durations, offsets, and turn restrictions. Amber and all-red clear the junction; a conflicting phase holds traffic safely.

Add stops on roads. With Transit route, click stops in order, then Finish route. Buses serve a loop, wait to board, and take passengers to reachable downstream destinations. Select a stop, bus, or route for details.

Understand the results

Live charts report measured arrivals, travel times, queues, and speed. Emissions are an idle / movement proxy, not a calibrated air-quality model. Overlays expose demand, transit access, illegal turns, components, and corridor congestion. Mode comparison runs two 6-minute trials using your network and seed.

Keyboard & persistence

Space pause · . step 1 s · V inspect · R road · H pan · T signal · O roundabout · Z land use · P destination · S stop · B route · I closure · X delete · F fit · Esc cancel.

Ctrl Z / Ctrl Shift Z undo / redo. Autosave stays in this browser; named saves and JSON preserve a complete snapshot.
