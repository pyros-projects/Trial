Welcome to the laboratory.

Materia is a tiny world with real consequences. Every cell carries its own heat, motion, fuel, electrical charge, and chemistry. Add an element, then follow the chain reaction.

Start with a little alchemy

Lava + water makes steam and cooled stone. Fire + wood or oil consumes fuel and spreads heat. Electricity + metal travels through connected conductors, heats them, and can ignite explosives. Acid + metal corrodes the metal and releases gas; stone resists longer. Plants + water grow and use the water. Dissolved salt changes water's freezing point.

Make your mark

Drag to paint continuously. Right-drag erases; hold Alt to sample. Heat and cool change temperatures, wind gives particles momentum, and explosions push materials away. Wall draws resistant boundaries; fill replaces a connected area. Arrow keys move a keyboard brush; Enter paints at its position.

Look under the surface

Use the view selector to see actual temperature, velocity, density, charge, fuel, reaction activity, or update order. In the activity view, bright cells are reacting or accumulating corrosion. Settings change the real simulation.

Keep your worlds

Save downloads a compact JSON file with the entire world, physics settings, seed, sources, and brush. Load restores it exactly and pauses for inspection. PNG exports the selected diagnostic view. Local autosave keeps a snapshot every 15 seconds, when enabled. Reset rebuilds the selected preset using the world seed. Clear removes all particles and emitters.

Load state JSON

Paste a state exported by Materia. Your current world stays intact if the file is invalid.
