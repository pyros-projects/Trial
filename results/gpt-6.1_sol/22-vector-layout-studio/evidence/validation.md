These failures were observed during development and were not counted as passes:

| Observed failure | Cause and repair | Retest |

| Escape during first-anchor drag crashed rendering and interrupted Reset | Cancellation restored an empty path draft; path rendering accessed its first nonexistent anchor. Restore `null` for the first anchor and guard empty path data. | First-anchor Escape, fresh Reset and empty-history draft Undo passed with no errors. Historical error log is retained in `historical-errors.json`. |

| Range pointer cancellation left partial opacity | Scrub lacked cancellation handling. Restore its transaction on pointercancel/lost capture/focus loss. | Cancelled scrub restored the entire document/history; completed scrub remained one step. |
