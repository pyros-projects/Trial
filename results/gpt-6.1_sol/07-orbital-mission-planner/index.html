Explore the system

Select a body in the rail or the canvas. Drag empty space to pan; use the wheel, pinch, or zoom controls to move between scales. Fit shows the whole system. Focus gives the selected spacecraft's local primary context. In the selected-body frame, the selected body is the origin. The initial view is primary-centered for a useful local overview; selecting a body updates the origin.

Plan, then fly

Add a maneuver from the inspector or click the future timeline. Set a signed prograde and radial delta-v, or inertial Cartesian components. The green prediction is the unmodified flight; the amber path includes scheduled burns. Changes preview immediately and apply to the live spacecraft at the specified mission time. Click a node to edit it. Predictions include all active spacecraft.

Change the initial conditions

Pause, then drag a spacecraft to set its position. Drag the small square at the end of its velocity vector to change velocity. Use the pencil for exact inertial coordinates, mass, radius and display size. State edits reset the energy baseline. Save a checkpoint to return to an exact earlier state, or restart the current scenario.

Read the instruments

Position and velocity values follow the selected frame, including subtraction of angular velocity in rotating space. Orbital elements use primary-relative inertial motion and are osculating estimates in this interacting system. Energy and momentum errors cover the entire system, with intentional impulses and merge losses subtracted. Periapsis and apoapsis are center-to-center radii. Predicted encounters are estimates from sampled motion; shorten the integration step or increase samples to refine them.

Units & numerical model

Distance u, time t and mass m are normalized units, with G=1 initially. Every body perturbs every other body. A second-order velocity-Verlet integrator uses an encounter safety cap and splits steps at burn events. Softening is configurable. Error can rise in strong encounters or at coarse steps; reduce the timestep if needed. This educational model has instantaneous burns, planar motion and no atmosphere or fuel model.
