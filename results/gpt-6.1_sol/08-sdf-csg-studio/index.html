The stack evaluates top to bottom. Subtraction exposes the cutter’s material; intersection keeps only the shared volume. Smooth operations blend within their radius. Picking uses the actual ray-marched surface.

On touch screens: drag to orbit, pinch to zoom, and use two fingers to pan. Open Scene and Inspector from the viewport bar.
Non-uniform scale uses a conservative distance bound. Glass and reflections are real-time approximations. Ray steps are sampled at the viewport center or the last picked pixel.
