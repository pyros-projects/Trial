CLICK Aim and plan a route

On touch screens, drag the stick or tap a destination. Tap E to interact, RUN to toggle speed, and USE to deploy the selected tool. A route waits at closed doors; open them to continue. A keyboard movement overrides the route.

Security. Terminals grant an access code, temporarily loop camera feeds, and calm the alarm. Locked doors can also be picked: stay still while the progress ring completes. Doors can be closed to break line of sight.

Patrols. Amber cones mean suspicion. Red means pursuit. A guard remembers where they last saw or heard you, searches that area, then returns to their route. Walls, doors, solid furniture, and smoke occlude vision. Running and bumps create real acoustic events; nearby guards can hear through walls at reduced range.

Your tools. Distraction lands toward your pointer (or your facing direction) and makes repeated noise pulses. Smoke forms a temporary sight barrier around you. EMP disables cameras within 300 units for 12 seconds and unlocks nearby security doors. All tools have finite charges and a shared short cooldown.

Extraction. Retrieve the blue dossier in the upper-right vault, then interact with the green loading zone. You must break immediate pursuit before leaving. Optional intel improves your score.
