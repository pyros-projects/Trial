FIELD NOTES

A laboratory for your curiosity.

Start with a question

Why do two waves sometimes disappear? Place two sources, select one, and change its phase. Bright bands reinforce one another; dark nodal lines reveal destructive interference.

Build, move, measure

Choose a tool, then click or drag in the field. Reflectors and absorbers follow your stroke. Drag a line emitter, slit wall, array, or lens to set its extent. Medium paints a region with the chosen refractive index.

Array steering is relative to the perpendicular direction of the drawn row; focus distance places a target along that direction. Use Select to move sources, probes, and structures. Click an object to edit it in the inspector. A selected line has endpoint handles. Erase removes material under the brush and any touched sources or probes. Delete removes the selected object.

Read the field

Amplitude shows signed displacement. Intensity averages u² over the persistence interval. Phase uses displacement and local velocity at the reference source frequency. Gradient measures spatial change; energy flow shows the averaged vector −uₜ∇u. Medium shows n, with local wave speed c/n.

The equation underneath

uₜₜ + 2γuₜ = (c/n)²∇²u + s

A centered, five-point finite-difference grid advances the continuous scalar field. Reflectors pin displacement to zero. Absorbers dissipate energy; an edge sponge reduces outgoing reflections. Periodic boundaries join opposite edges.

Keep it stable

The Courant ratio is (cₘₐₓ Δt / Δx)√2. Requested timesteps are automatically limited to a ratio of 0.92. Fine grids and fast media need smaller steps. Substeps control simulation speed, independently of stability. Wavelengths below eight grid cells receive a resolution warning.

Measure honestly

Probes sample the actual field. Amplitude is √2 × RMS over the recent history; dominant frequency and phase use a Fourier scan over 0.2–5 Hz. Allow several cycles for estimates to settle. Phase is referenced to simulation time, and is an estimate for mixed-frequency fields.

Save your work

Save scene stores geometry and settings in this browser. Restore or export/import JSON using the ⋯ menu. Saved scenes rebuild the wave field from zero. Clear field resets time and probe history. Reset scene restores the current preset.
