Life, under observation.

Grazers seek renewable plants and flee hunters. Hunters pursue prey and scavenge. Decomposers recycle carcasses into fertile soil. Movement, digestion, temperature, age, and reproduction all have an energy cost.

Click a creature to inspect its live sensors, inherited steering weights, genotype, and ancestry. Use the habitat tools to draw changes. Drag to pan; use the wheel or pinch to zoom. Protected areas prevent predation and disasters, while normal metabolism continues.
