A field guide to the invisible


Follow a photon
Every pixel sends a ray backward from the camera. Gravity changes its path. Some rays fall through the event horizon; others cross the disk or escape into a procedural star field. Light from the far side of the disk bends above and below the shadow, revealing multiple images of the same structure.


The geometry
Distances are in reference Schwarzschild radii, rₛ. At lensing strength 1 and spin 0, the photon sphere is at 1.5 × the horizon radius and the critical impact parameter is approximately 2.598 × that radius. The visible shadow is larger than the event horizon.
d²x / dλ² = −(3/2) rₛ L² x / |x|⁵
The renderer uses adaptive velocity-Verlet integration of the Schwarzschild spatial null-ray equation. L is the ray's angular momentum. Spin adds a small frame-dragging inspired term; this is an educational approximation, not a full Kerr solution. Lensing strength and horizon size are independently adjustable experiments.


The disk and its light
A finite-thickness emitting disk is intersected along each curved ray. Radial cooling, procedural gas density and differential rotation shape the emission. Gravitational redshift lowers the frequency near the horizon. A relativistic Doppler approximation shifts and beams emission from approaching gas.
Frequency factor g = gravitational factor × Doppler factor. Values above 1 are blueshifted; values below 1 are redshifted. Temperature colors are a visible blackbody approximation, rather than the true X-ray spectrum of a hot accretion disk. Bloom is an image treatment.


Inspect the calculation
Switch visualization to see step count, accumulated turning angle, frequency factor, disk coordinates, minimum ray distance, or escape status. Cyan rays escape, coral rays are captured, and magenta rays exhaust the integration budget. Increase maximum steps or ray step size if too many rays exhaust the budget. The footer averages nine camera rays until you select a pixel. The ray inspector uses the same equations, camera and integration settings as the shader, with no antialiasing jitter.
