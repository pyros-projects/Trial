RHYTHM SCORING

Every shot, dash and graze is judged against the musical transport: PERFECT (±12% of a beat), GOOD (±25%), or miss. Perfect shots deal double damage and erase light bullets; perfect dashes refund cooldown. Grazing (near-miss) bullets builds combo — grazes within ±70ms of a beat pay a bonus. Combo raises your multiplier (up to ×8) and resets when hit. The game is fully playable off-beat — rhythm pays.

THE CONDUCTOR

Survive four phases. Each phase changes the bullet patterns and the arrangement of the live-synthesized soundtrack. Phase transitions clear the screen. Beat lane at the bottom shows the transport; the ring around your ship contracts on every beat.
