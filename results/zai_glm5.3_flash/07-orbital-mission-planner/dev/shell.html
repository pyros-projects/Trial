Orbital Sandbox

N-body gravity with a velocity-Verlet (symplectic), RK4, or adaptive Dormand–Prince integrator. Every body pulls on every other; spacecraft are (nearly) massless test particles.

Mouse / touch — drag empty space: pan · wheel / pinch: zoom · click a body: select.
While paused — drag a selected spacecraft's ▲ position or its velocity-arrow tip to edit the state; the prediction re-plots live.
Maneuvers — “＋ Node” then click the predicted path; edit prograde/radial ΔV in the Mission panel. Nodes fire automatically when the clock reaches them.

Keys — Space pause · . step · R restart · C checkpoint · V rewind · F follow · N node · 1/2/3 frames · +/− warp

Frames — Inertial, body-centered, and co-rotating (two-body) frames transform trails, predictions, and velocity vectors coherently. In the rotating frame the co-orbiting moon appears stationary.
